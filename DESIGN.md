## Brand & Style

This design system radiates warmth, safe exploration, and joyful curiosity. Designed for early learners, educators, and mindful parents, the visual tone balances playful delight with clean, contemporary clarity. 

The aesthetic merges modern warmth with tactile softness:
- **Base Canvas:** Soft, nurturing tones replacing sterile whites with delicate warm creams and airy atmospheric blues.
- **Micro-Delights:** Pill containers, buoyant micro-badges, and interactive cards that invite touch and discovery without cognitive overwhelm.
- **Clarity & Safety:** High legibility paired with obvious affordances, generous tap targets, and recognizable parent/teacher safety signifiers.
- **Restraint within Vibrancy:** Joyful accents (coral, mint, sunshine, lavender) are applied methodically to focal points and interactive states, allowing ample whitespace to anchor young eyes.

## Colors

The palette establishes an encouraging, optimistic atmosphere with strong contrast ratios that meet WCAG AAA standards for young readers.

- **Primary (`#FF6B4A` - Joyful Coral):** Drives key actions, celebratory states, and energetic highlights.
- **Secondary (`#4EA8DE` - Sky Blue):** Directs navigation, informational highlights, and friendly utility interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#FFC043` - Sunshine Yellow):** Rewards progress, achievements, stars, and playful accents.
- **Supporting Accents:**
  - *Mint Green (`#4EBA87`):* Verification, correct answers, parent-verified gates, and growth metrics.
  - *Soft Lavender (`#9D8DF1`):* Creative tasks, bedtime audio, and calming rest states.
- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - *Base Surface:* `#FAF8F5` (Warm Cream).
  - *Alternate Canvas:* `#F0F7FF` (Sky Tint) for thematic sections or playful cards.
  - *Surface Elevated:* `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) with tinted ambient drops.
- **Neutrals:**
  - *Heading & Deep Text (`#2B2D42` - Soft Ink):* Gentle on young eyes, eschewing harsh `#000000`.
  - *Muted Body (`#5C6079`):* Secondary text, timestamps, and subtle instructional notes.
  - *Borders & Dividers (`#EAE5DC`):* Delicate warm outlines.

## Typography

The typography couples the welcoming, rounded terminals of **Quicksand** with the rhythmic geometric clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Display & Headings (Quicksand):** Curved letterforms mimic early-childhood printing, making reading intuitive and non-intimidating. Always render in semi-bold (`600`) or bold (`700`).
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Balances playful headlines with superior open counters, distinct ascenders/descenders, and crisp punctuation required for instructions and reading practice.
- **Rhythm & Line Height:** Line heights are deliberately expanded (1.5x - 1.6x) to facilitate visual tracking for early readers and ease scanning for adults on mobile devices.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts prioritize breathing room, content chunking, and tactile touch targets.

- **Grid System:** A responsive 12-column fluid grid on desktop (max width: `1240px`), 8-column on tablet, and 4-column on mobile.
- **Whitespace Strategy:** Sections employ ample vertical buffers (`space-xl` to `4rem`) to reduce visual clutter and isolate learning activities into bite-sized missions.
- **Touch-First Insets:** Buttons and interactive elements maintain minimum hit areas of `48px` (ideally `56px` for kid-facing interactions) with uniform inner padding (`space-sm` vertical, `space-md` to `space-lg` horizontal).
- **Responsive Flow:** Horizontal module carousels gracefully degrade into stacked rounded cards on small viewports with edge-to-edge margins (`margin-mobile`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, diffuse ambient drop shadows that produce a floating, pillowy appearance instead of harsh architectural elevation.

- **Ambient Tints:** Shadows use a desaturated tone derived from the neutral ink (`#2B2D42`) at low opacities, enriched with warm undertones rather than cold grayscale:
  - *Level 0 (Flat/Inset):* Subtly recessed fields or track bars using `#F0ECE4`.
  - *Level 1 (Cards & Modules):* `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(43, 45, 66, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(43, 45, 66, 0.03)`.
  - *Level 2 (Floating Pills & Modals):* `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(43, 45, 66, 0.10), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(43, 45, 66, 0.04)`.
  - *Level 3 (Interactive Active States):* `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(255, 107, 74, 0.25)` for celebratory elements, casting a colored glow in the primary hue.
- **Tactile Edge Details:** Combine soft shadows with a micro `1px` translucent border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` on top edge, `rgba(43, 45, 66, 0.04)` on base edge) to give cards physical presence.

## Shapes

The design language embraces an ultra-soft, friendly geometry:

- **Pill Philosophy (`ROUND_FULL`):** All interactive controls, badges, chips, search inputs, and navigation pills are rendered with full radial endcaps (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Cards & Containers (`rounded-3xl` / `24px - 32px`):** Activity panels, parent dashboards, and modal dialogs feature exaggerated corner roundings, eliminating sharp corners.
- **Floating Micro-Badges:** Small circular or rounded-pill tags that sit overlapping container corners to denote status, rewards, or topic tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** High-contrast `#FF6B4A` background with crisp white text (`label-md`), full pill shape, generous horizontal padding (`space-lg`), and subtle bounce animation on active press (`scale: 0.96`).
- **Secondary Pill:** Pale tint background (`#F0F7FF`), Sky Blue text (`#4EA8DE`), and no heavy border.
- **Safety / Parent Pin Action:** Neutral muted tone with a lock icon, requiring a deliberate press-and-hold or math gate interaction.

### Chips & Micro-Badges
- **Floating Category Badges:** Small pill tags (`label-sm`) with soft tinted backgrounds (e.g., `#FFF4E5` for Math, `#E8F8F0` for Science) positioned slightly overlapping the top-left or top-right boundary of cards.
- **Reward Badges:** Circular icons with Tertiary Yellow (`#FFC043`) rings, star iconography, and animated shimmer on completion.

### Interactive Activity Cards
- Built on `rounded-3xl` containers with clean `#FFFFFF` surfaces over the `#FAF8F5` background.
- Include a high-aspect visual/illustration area, an expressive Quicksand title (`headline-sm`), progress bar pill, and floating status chip.
- Generous internal padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`).

### Input Fields
- Fully rounded pill containers with a thick `2px` border defaulting to `#EAE5DC`, transitioning to `#4EA8DE` on focus with a matching soft diffuse focus ring.
- Large, legible placeholder text with integrated friendly icons positioned on the leading edge.

### Checkboxes, Radios & Switches
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Scaled up (`24px x 24px` minimum) with smooth rounded borders (`8px` for checkbox, circular for radios). Checked state fills with Mint Green (`#4EBA87`) displaying an exaggerated, chunky checkmark.
- **Toggles:** Oversized pill switches with playful bouncy thumb transitions.

### Child Safety & Parent Gates
- Dedicated floating shield badge marked with a lock symbol.
- Parent-mode verification modals require written multiplication prompts or biometric confirmation, presented in an uncluttered, high-legibility modal card.
